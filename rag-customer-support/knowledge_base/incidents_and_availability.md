# Incidents and service availability

## Incident severity
A suspected security incident, cross-tenant data exposure, or widespread production outage must be escalated immediately to the on-call incident commander. A single-user usability issue with no security or availability impact follows the normal support queue.

## Status communications
Use the official status page for confirmed service availability updates. Do not speculate about root cause or recovery time. Only the incident commander or designated communications lead may publish incident updates.

## Evidence collection
For an incident report, collect the approximate UTC time, affected workspace ID, request or correlation ID, endpoint, and a concise description. Do not collect passwords, API keys, session cookies, or full payment data. Preserve logs according to the incident-response policy.
