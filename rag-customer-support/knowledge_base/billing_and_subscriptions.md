# Billing and subscriptions

## Invoice questions
Customers can download invoices from Workspace Settings, then Billing, then Invoice History. Only workspace owners and billing administrators can access invoices. Support agents must not send full payment card details or authentication secrets by email.

## Failed payments
When a payment fails, check the invoice status and payment provider event before asking the customer to retry. Do not promise that a payment has been captured until the billing system confirms it. If the invoice remains unpaid after the provider reports success, escalate to Billing Operations with the invoice ID and provider event ID.

## Refund requests
Refund eligibility depends on the customer's contract and the applicable refund policy. Support can explain the review process but must not promise approval. Route refund decisions to Billing Operations and record the case ID.
