# Data handling and privacy

## Support data minimization
Collect only the information required to resolve the support request. Redact access tokens, passwords, session cookies, payment card data, and unnecessary personal information before attaching logs.

## Data deletion requests
Verify the requester and workspace authority before initiating a deletion request. Support must create a privacy case and route it to the Privacy Operations team. Do not claim data has been deleted until the deletion workflow confirms completion.

## Retention and exports
Data-retention and export questions must be answered using the customer's contract and current workspace configuration. If those facts are unavailable, explain that the answer needs verification and route the request to Privacy Operations rather than inventing a retention period.
